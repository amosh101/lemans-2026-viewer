Recent Winners (2015-2025)

The last 11 years of Le Mans. Toyota dominated 2018-2022. Ferrari took over 2023-2025. Earl Bamber (2 wins), Fernando Alonso (2 wins), Sébastien Buemi (3 wins) lead the modern era.

Toyota 5-peat (2018-2022)

Toyota Gazoo Racing won Le Mans 5 years in a row: Alonso/Buemi/Nakajima in 2018-2019, Buemi/Nakajima/Hartley in 2020, Conway/Kobayashi/López in 2021, Buemi/Hartley/Hirakawa in 2022. Then Ferrari ended the streak in 2023 with the 499P, exactly 50 years after Ferrari's last Le Mans win (1973 P4).

Ferrari 3-peat (2023-2025)

Ferrari AF Corse has won 3 in a row: Calado/Giovinazzi/Pier Guidi in 2023, Fuoco/Molina/Nielsen in 2024, and Hanson/Kubica/Yifei Ye in 2025 — with a customer #83 car, the biggest upset in recent Le Mans history.
